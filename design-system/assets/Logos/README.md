The mzizi mark is seven nodes, a hub and six roots, joined by edges into one network. It shows mzizi as the root system connecting every product in the ecosystem. It is drawn in **hematite**, mzizi's ecosystem mineral (the foundation, the substrate). Gold belongs to nyuchi; the mark is never gold.

- `mzizi-mark-light.svg` / `.png`: nodes in `#546e7a` (hematite's light value), for light grounds (`background`, `card`). 5.1:1 on `#faf9f5`.
- `mzizi-mark-dark.svg` / `.png`: nodes in `#90a4ae` (hematite's dark value), for dark grounds. 7.1:1 on `#141414`.
- The edges are the node colour at 55% opacity, 5 units wide, with round caps. The nodes have radius 20, and the hub radius 22, in a 500-unit square.
- The SVGs are the masters. The source files are `components/registry/assets/mzizi-mark-{light,dark}.svg` in mzizi-registry, installable with `npx shadcn@latest add https://api.mzizi.dev/v1/ui/mzizi-mark-light` (or `-dark`).
- Pick the version that matches the theme. Never recolour, redraw or rotate the mark, and never add a node.
- Keep clear space of at least one node's diameter around the mark.
- Pair it with the lowercase wordmark `mzizi`: Noto Serif bold for display, Noto Sans otherwise.
