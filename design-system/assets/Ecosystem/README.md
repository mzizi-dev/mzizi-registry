Marks of sister brands in the bundu ecosystem. None of them is the mzizi mark, so never use one to stand for mzizi.

- `bundu-mark-light.png` / `bundu-mark-dark.png`: bundu, the ecosystem. A copper ring around an organic, root-shaped inner field with a crosshair of lines and nodes. On light grounds the ring is copper with a navy field and white lines; on dark grounds the ring is tan with a pale-blue field and navy lines. Its mineral is `copper`.
- `nyuchi-bee-light.png` / `nyuchi-bee-dark.png`: nyuchi ("bee"), the infrastructure and enterprise layer. A bee silhouette in brown on light grounds and in honey-yellow on dark grounds. Its mineral is `gold`.
- `mukoko-mark-light.png` / `mukoko-mark-dark.png`: mukoko ("beehive"), Africa's super app. A honeycomb of seven hexagons, one for each of the Seven Minerals. `tanzanite`, mukoko's mineral, sits at the centre. Around it, clockwise from the top: `sodalite`, `terracotta`, `cobalt`, `gold`, `malachite`, `copper`. The light version uses each mineral's light value with cream seams on `#faf9f5`. The dark version uses the dark values with near-black seams. Never reorder the cells or swap their colours.

Use each mark as supplied, in the version that matches the theme. Write every wordmark in lowercase.

The source files are in `components/registry/assets/` in mzizi-registry. These marks are raster PNGs for now. Once vector masters exist, each becomes an installable registry item, as the mzizi mark already is.
