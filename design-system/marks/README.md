# Brand marks

The official marks of the bundu ecosystem, each with a light version (for `background` and `card` in light) and a dark version (for dark grounds). All are 500×500 PNGs. SVG masters are not yet in the repo.

| Brand  | Light                   | Dark                   | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------ | ----------------------- | ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| mzizi  | `mzizi-mark-light.png`  | `mzizi-mark-dark.png`  | Seven nodes, a hub and six roots, joined into one network. Nodes are `#5d4037` on light grounds and `#ffd740` on dark (`gold`'s values), with the edges at half strength.                                                                           |
| bundu  | `bundu-mark-light.png`  | `bundu-mark-dark.png`  | A ring around a root-shaped field crossed by lines and nodes. On light grounds: a copper ring, a navy field and white lines. On dark grounds: a tan ring, a pale-blue field and navy lines.                                                         |
| nyuchi | `nyuchi-bee-light.png`  | `nyuchi-bee-dark.png`  | The bee (nyuchi). Brown on light grounds, honey-yellow on dark.                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| mukoko | `mukoko-mark-light.png` | `mukoko-mark-dark.png` | The hive (mukoko): a honeycomb of the Seven Minerals. `tanzanite` is at the centre; around it, clockwise from the top, are `sodalite`, `terracotta`, `cobalt`, `gold`, `malachite` and `copper`. Each cell uses that mineral's light or dark value. |

## Rules

- Use each mark as supplied, in the version that matches the theme.
- Never recolour, redraw, rotate or re-arrange a mark. Never add a node or reorder a cell.
- Keep clear space of at least one node's (or cell's) width around the mark.
- Never use one brand's mark to stand for another.
- Wordmarks are lowercase: `mzizi`, `bundu`, `nyuchi`, `mukoko`.
