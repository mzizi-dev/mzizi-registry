Architecture is designed for small screens, limited battery, constrained data plans, and the hardware reality of the African smartphone market — including Android, iOS, and critically, Huawei HarmonyOS devices.

## Sizes follow Apple on touch and step down as screens grow

Owner decision, 2026-11-01: mobile stays the design centre, but a laptop or an iPad is not a phone. Touch sizes follow Apple's Human Interface Guidelines, raised to the Bundu floor (a 49px hit region; 17px body), and a density layer in the token layer draws controls and body text smaller for a fine pointer on a wide screen, down to the desktop tier of the floor: a 28px hit region and 15px body (owner rulings 2026-11-02 and 2026-11-12, #660: the Bundu floor has two pointer tiers). It is defined once as media queries on the size variables; a component never branches on screen size. Density is visual only: a wide screen is not evidence of a fast network, so loading strategy, lazy sections and memory-pressure behaviour above do not change with it.

| Layer        | Query                                               | Control drawn at                                | Hit region | Body | Caption |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ---- | ------- |
| Phone, touch | the defaults                                        | 49px (large 56px)                               | 49px       | 17px | 14px    |
| Tablet       | `min-width: 48rem`                                  | 49px (touched)                                  | 49px       | 17px | 14px    |
| Desktop      | `min-width: 64rem`, `pointer: fine`, `hover: hover` | 36px (32 small, 40 large; fields 36px and 32px) | 28px       | 15px | 14px    |

The Bundu floor in every layer: a hit region on every interactive control (49px on touch, 28px on a desktop, where the drawn box is the hit region), body text 17px on touch and 15px on a desktop, captions and secondary labels 14px everywhere, zoom and `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
